New smart feature

e-Salary Guard.

Take-home pay worked out from attendance, overtime and leave, and explained to every employee by WhatsApp the moment a deduction appears. Not on payday.

Attendance to pay

Calculated, not guessed.

Lateness, absence, leave and completed overtime flow straight into each person's take-home pay, recalculated whenever anything changes.

Early warning

No surprises on payday.

Every deduction sends a WhatsApp alert with the reason, the date and the new estimate, so mistakes are caught before money moves.

People in control

AI suggests. HR decides.

SEA-LION reads leave reasons and suggests paid or unpaid. HR approves, can override, and every step lands in the activity log.
